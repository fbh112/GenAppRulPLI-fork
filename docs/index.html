GenApp PL/I General Insurance Application Wiki

Architectural and program-level knowledge base for the GenApp General Insurance application, implemented in Enterprise PL/I on CICS, DB2 and VSAM. It covers the customer and policy domains, the user-interface transactions, the batch utilities and the supporting infrastructure.

Application Documentation
Overview & Architecture
Project Overview & Program Inventory Introduction to the GenApp PL/I application, with a full inventory of programs grouped by role: UI, business logic, DB2, VSAM, batch and utilities. Customer Domain Architecture Architecture of the customer domain: how the menu, business-logic, DB2 and VSAM layers interact for customer inquiry, add and update. It also covers how the programs relate to each other. Customer Domain Migration Risks & Impact Analysis Impact analysis of migrating the customer domain, including risks and affected programs.
User Interface Transactions
Customer Maintenance Menu (SSC1) The LGTESTC1 interactive BMS menu for customer inquiry, add and update. It covers the ENQ/DEQ-protected GENACNTL queue update and error display. Policy Maintenance Menus (SSP1–SSP4) Interactive BMS menu transactions for motor (SSP1), endowment (SSP2), house (SSP3) and commercial (SSP4) policies. They route to the inquiry, add, delete and update programs.
Customer Domain Programs
Customer Inquiry Customer inquiry flow: the LGICUS01 business-logic entry point and the LGICDB01 DB2 retrieval program. Customer Add & Onboarding Customer creation flow: LGACUS01 and LGACDB01 (DB2 insert and customer number assignment), LGACDB02 (security record) and LGACVS01 (VSAM write). Customer Update Customer update flow: LGUCUS01 (entry point), LGUCDB01 (DB2 update) and LGUCVS01 (VSAM rewrite). Customer VSAM Programs Programs that access the KSDSCUST VSAM file: LGACVS01 (add), LGUCVS01 (update) and LGICVS01 (random customer selection).
Policy Domain Programs
Policy Inquiry Policy inquiry flow: LGIPOL01 (business-logic front end) and LGIPDB01 (DB2 retrieval of endowment, house, motor, commercial and claim data). Policy Add Policy creation flow: LGAPOL01 (orchestration and optional ODM rules) and LGAPDB01 (DB2 insert with pricing, discount, surcharge and commission logic). It also covers the LGAPVS01 VSAM write. Policy Update Policy update flow: LGUPOL01 (validation and delegation), LGUPDB01 (DB2 update with timestamp lost-update protection) and LGUPVS01 (VSAM update). Policy Delete Policy deletion flow: LGDPOL01 (front end), LGDPDB01 (DB2 delete with cascade) and LGDPVS01 (VSAM delete). Policy VSAM (KSDSPOLY) Programs Programs that access the KSDSPOLY policy file: add (LGAPVS01), update (LGUPVS01), delete (LGDPVS01) and generic GTEQ browse (LGIPVS01). It covers the composite key structure and return codes.
Batch Processing
Batch Customer Reporting Batch customer report generation: the LGBATCH driver, the LGBATCHF formatting subroutine and the LGBATDB2 test driver. Batch Motor Policy Extract The LGMOTOUT batch program, which extracts motor policies from the DB2 MOTOR table into a fixed-length sequential file.
Infrastructure, Logging & Statistics
Error Logging (LGSTSQ) The shared LGSTSQ logging utility that writes to the CSMT transient data queue and the GENAERRS temporary storage queue. It also covers the diagnostic message conventions used by the other programs. Environment Setup (LGSETUP) Initialization of the GenApp CICS environment: temporary storage queues, the customer number counter and statistics counters. Statistics Collection (LGASTAT1 & LGWEBST5) Statistics pipeline: LGASTAT1 increments named counters per request and outcome. LGWEBST5 harvests the counters and writes absolute and delta values to temporary storage queues every minute.
