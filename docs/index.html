GenApp PL/I Insurance Application Knowledge Base

Comprehensive technical architecture, domain workflows, data persistence models, batch processing, and operational infrastructure documentation for the General Insurance (GenApp) PL/I CICS/DB2/VSAM system.

Application Documentation
Architecture & System Overview
Application Architecture & Component Inventory High-level architectural overview of the GenApp PL/I CICS multi-tiered system, layering conventions, communication area structures, and full component catalog across customer and policy domains. DB2 & VSAM Data Storage Architecture Cross-reference mapping of DB2 relational tables, VSAM KSDS files, dual-write synchronization patterns, and program-level data dependencies across all insurance operations. Customer Domain Migration & Risk Analysis Impact analysis and risk assessment for modernizing and decoupling the customer domain, evaluating data integrity risks, dual-store consistency, and middleware dependencies.
Customer Domain Services
Customer Presentation & Business Orchestration Interactive BMS terminal menu (transaction SSC1) and core business logic layer handling customer onboarding, profile inquiry, lifecycle administration, and updates. Customer DB2 & Security Persistence Back-end DB2 data access services for inserting, querying, updating customer records and managing credential security in the CUSTOMER and CUSTOMER_SECURE tables. Customer VSAM Data Services KSDS file-handling routines for KSDSCUST maintaining dual-store parity, including record insertion, update with exclusive locking, and random customer key resolution.
Policy Domain Services
Policy Presentation & Business Orchestration CICS BMS menu transactions (SSP1, SSP2, SSP3, SSP4) and business dispatchers coordinating add, inquiry, update, and deletion operations across Motor, Endowment, House, and Commercial policies. Policy DB2 Persistence & Rating Engine Relational database operations for policies and claims, incorporating premium surcharges, multi-policy bundling discounts, broker commission rules, and optimistic locking validations. Policy VSAM Storage Services VSAM KSDS file maintenance routines for KSDSPOLY providing composite-key record creation, generic browsing, read-for-update rewrites, and deletion handling.
Batch Processing & Utilities
Customer Batch Reporting & Test Drivers Standalone batch programs and subroutines executing outside CICS to query customer data from DB2, format report lines, and generate batch output files. Motor Policy Batch Extraction Utility Sequential fixed-length record extraction utility querying the DB2 MOTOR policy table to export vehicle attributes, policy coverage, and accident history.
System Infrastructure & Operations
Environment Setup & Queue Management Application environment reset and configuration program initializing TSQs (control, statistics, errors), customer number ranges, and named counter pools. Transaction Statistics & Telemetry Pipeline Event-processing adapter and continuous background statistics collector harvesting CICS named counters and tracking real-time delta rates across business operations. Centralized Error Logging & Diagnostics Reusable error logging routine outputting diagnostic dumps, SQLCODEs, and response codes to Transient Data Queues (CSMT) and Temporary Storage Queues (GENAERRS).
